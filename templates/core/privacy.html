Privacy Policy

GramSetu V2 respects citizen privacy. User data such as name, phone number, and ward details are stored securely in PostgreSQL database strictly for Panchayat governance and service delivery.

Private documents uploaded for service applications are stored with strict object-level access authorization. Only the authorized applicant and verified Panchayat Administrators can access applicant files.
